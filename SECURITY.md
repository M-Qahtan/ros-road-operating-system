# Security Policy

ROS processes sensitive location, incident, safety and evidence data. Security issues must not be disclosed through public issues.

## Baseline controls

- least privilege and role-based access;
- MFA for operational users;
- TLS in transit and encryption at rest;
- secrets outside source control;
- immutable audit records;
- signed evidence uploads and access logging;
- dependency and secret scanning;
- data minimization and retention controls.

## Prohibited data

Never commit real personal data, medical information, credentials, production evidence or unredacted incident records.
