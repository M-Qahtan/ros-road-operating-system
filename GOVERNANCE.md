# ROS Engineering Governance

## Decision rights

- **Founder:** mission, scope, risk acceptance and external commitments.
- **Product:** backlog priority and acceptance criteria.
- **Architecture:** module boundaries, technical standards and ADRs.
- **Safety:** operational playbooks, escalation rules and automation prohibitions.
- **Security/Data:** privacy, data access and model governance.

## Escalation rule

Only decisions that alter human safety policy, legal exposure, governmental authority, material budget or MVP scope require founder approval.
