# ROS Master Context

ROS is not a reporting app. It is an operational layer for roads that creates one trusted RoadEvent from fragmented signals, protects people first, coordinates response and tracks the road until recovery.

## Founder intent

Build a globally extensible road operating system, beginning with a controlled Riyadh MVP that can be explained, audited and evaluated by government, safety and investment stakeholders.

## MVP constraint

No capability enters the MVP unless it materially improves human safety, RoadEvent reliability, operational response or auditability.
