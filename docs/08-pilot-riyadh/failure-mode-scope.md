# Failure-mode suite scope

The automated suite is deterministic and simulation-only. It validates fail-closed behavior, bounded retry and idempotency, authorization, evidence integrity, escalation deadlines, append-only late evidence, and safe degradation within the Riyadh MVP.

It does not represent real government integration, medical diagnosis, legal fault determination, autonomous dispatch, autonomous severity downgrade, autonomous case resolution, or autonomous road closure/reopening. Human authorization remains mandatory for safety-critical S3/S4 decisions and all legally or medically consequential actions.
