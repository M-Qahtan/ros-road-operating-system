# Riyadh MVP Scope

## In scope

- consented identity and device registration;
- user incident report and simulated device signals;
- signal validation, idempotency and normalization;
- RoadEvent creation, correlation, severity and state machine;
- automatic safety conversation and no-response escalation;
- operator dashboard, timeline and commands;
- nearby hazard notifications;
- simulated agency adapters;
- evidence metadata, hashing and access audit;
- operational metrics and incident reports.

## Out of scope

- legal fault determination;
- medical diagnosis;
- official road closure or traffic-light control;
- production government dispatch;
- blockchain, vehicle passport and city digital twin;
- accuracy claims for predictive AI without field validation.
